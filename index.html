    ℹ️ Introduction

    Project DASIG (Digital Application System for Institutional Governance) is a web-based platform designed to streamline and modernize the application process for the Recognition, Renewal, and Establishment of Schools for both public and private educational institutions. The system provides a centralized, efficient, and transparent process that enables applicants and regulatory personnel to manage applications electronically from submission to approval.

    The platform simplifies every stage of the application process by allowing schools to submit the required documents online, monitor the status of their applications in real time, receive notifications regarding deficiencies or updates, and comply with regulatory requirements through a secure digital environment.

    For regulatory offices, the system enhances workflow efficiency by organizing applications, facilitating document validation, tracking compliance, managing evaluations, and generating reports needed for decision-making. By reducing manual paperwork and improving coordination among stakeholders, the system helps ensure that applications for school recognition, renewal, and establishment are processed accurately, consistently, and within the prescribed standards.

    Ultimately, Project DASIG aims to promote a faster, more accessible, and more reliable application process while supporting quality assurance in the establishment and continued operation of educational institutions, both public and private.
